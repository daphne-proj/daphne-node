# Supplied context contract

Treat project knowledge as an input to the workflow, not as skill content.

Before reasoning, inspect the sources supplied or identified by the user: connected pages, attachments, repository documentation, accepted decisions, code, tests, and current behavior. Load only what bears on the question.

Keep a source ledger:

- **Fact:** directly established by a cited source or observed behavior.
- **Resolved decision:** accepted by the authority identified for this task.
- **Assumption:** believed but not established; include support and a falsifier.
- **Unknown:** missing information that could change the outcome.
- **Proposal or interpretation:** a candidate conclusion without decision authority.

When sources conflict, report the conflict and their status instead of blending them. Preserve resolved decisions exactly. Treat research conclusions as evidence until the project's decision process accepts them.

If material context is unavailable, narrow the conclusion or ask for the specific source needed. Never fill the gap with product conventions remembered from another task.
